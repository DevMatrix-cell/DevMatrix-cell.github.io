# The Manojkumar Archive

## Direction

An editorial archive for a quietly ambitious technology builder. The experience uses the visual language of aged paper, heritage publishing, and private collections rather than a generic developer dashboard.

## Tokens

- Forest: `#252D25`
- Aged ivory: `#E9E1D1`
- Walnut: `#634638`
- Antique brass: `#9B8155`
- Espresso: `#292322`
- Oxblood: `#743E3C`
- Stone: `#A69D8D`
- Display: Cormorant Garamond
- Body: Manrope

## Composition

The page alternates ivory editorial sections with forest and espresso interludes. Project cards are asymmetric archive entries, and their dialogs expose verified engineering context rather than decorative claims. The hero uses a restrained DM monogram composition because no authentic portrait asset is available.

## Interaction

Theme preference, mobile navigation, project filtering, keyboard-accessible project case studies, scroll reveals, and local contact-draft saving are supported. The contact form does not claim delivery because no verified backend or inbox is configured.
